Practice

The Testownik rule

In a drill, a question leaves after you answer it correctly a set number of times (two by default). Each mistake adds one more repetition, up to four. Answers you mark as a guess, or give after opening the hint, do not count towards leaving.

Spaced review

A question you answer right, and are sure of, comes back after 1, then 2, then 3 and 5 days. Before a test the gaps shrink so that everything gets a last look the evening before. A miss brings it back the same day.

Recall first

Recall mode hides the options until you have an answer in mind. Pulling an answer from memory builds a stronger memory than recognising it among options.

Open questions

Some tests ask open questions. Answer out loud or on paper, reveal the model answer, then grade yourself: missed, partly or got it. Only “got it” moves the question forward.

Mock exams

Questions are drawn in proportion to each lecture's slide count, and those rated likely are drawn more often. The timer is a guide; you can finish after it runs out. The grade uses typical thresholds (50, 60, 70, 80 and 90 %); the real cut-offs may differ.

Your data and privacy

  • No accounts, no sign-up and no cookies.
  • No analytics, trackers or advertising, and no requests to other websites; even the fonts are served from this site.
  • Your progress, starred courses and settings are saved in this browser's local storage. They never leave your device and are not shared between devices.
  • Answers you type in an open-question mock exam stay in this browser too.
  • Clearing this site's data in your browser removes everything. Each course also has a reset button on its Progress tab.

About the material

Notes and questions are written from the lecture slides published for each course. Small slide excerpts are shown next to the notes for reference; they remain the property of their authors. The lecture files themselves are not hosted here: get them from the official course pages.

FAQ

Is this the official course portal?

No. TestClub is an independent study aid made by students. Official announcements, grades and materials are on the university's own course pages.

Why does a course have notes but few questions, or none yet?

It is the middle of the semester. Courses are filled in as lectures happen; upcoming lectures are listed as "Not available yet".

What does "likely on the test" mean?

A judgement from the slides: definitions, acronyms and numbers the slides stress, and question shapes seen in past tests where those are known. It is an estimate, not inside information.

Will my progress follow me to my phone?

No. Progress is stored only in the browser where you practise, which is what keeps the site free of accounts.

Can I use it offline?

Pages you have opened usually load from the browser cache, but a connection is needed to open a course for the first time.
