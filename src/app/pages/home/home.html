How it works

  1. 01

    Read the notes

    Each lecture is cut down to its key facts, formulas and common mix-ups, each one next to the slide it comes from.

  2. 02

    Practise until it sticks

    Wrong answers come back until you get them right. Spaced review then brings every question back on later days, timed to the test.

  3. 03

    Sit a mock exam

    A timed test drawn in proportion to the lectures, in the format of the real one, with every answer explained at the end.
